About Me

I am Tom Erick Makokha, an aspiring STEM educator specialising in Mathematics and Physics, on a mission to help Africa achieve Sustainable Development Goal 4 — quality education for all. I've taught under both the Competency-Based Curriculum (CBC) and the 8-4-4 system, and I design student-centred lessons using tools like GeoGebra and PhET Simulations to make abstract concepts tangible.

Alongside teaching, I've been building skills in web development, data analysis, and AI — because I believe the next leap in African education will come from educators who can also read data, build tools, and use technology to reach more learners than a single classroom allows. This portfolio brings both sides of that together: my teaching practice and the digital projects I'm building around it.
